Sushant Kamble

XR Researcher & Engineer · M.S., University of Texas at Austin

I build and evaluate immersive experiences across virtual, augmented, and mixed reality. My work spans VR environments for user studies on embodiment and presence, physical computing prototypes, and hands-on workshops that introduce newcomers to spatial computing.

Before XR, I spent over five years as a front-end engineer building accessible, scalable interfaces for SaaS products with React, React Native, and TypeScript.
