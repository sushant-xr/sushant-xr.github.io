Hi! I'm Sushant, an Engineer and XR Researcher preparing for Fall 2027 PhD applications. I currently work as a UX Engineer at Beheld Health with Professor Maninder (Mini) Kahlon.

I hold an M.S. in Information Studies from the University of Texas at Austin, where I developed a strong interest in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Extended Reality (XR) from a research and interaction design perspective.

Alongside my full-time role, I've been pursuing independent XR research projects, investigating novel interaction techniques that bridge physical and virtual environments. My research interests include multimodal haptics, wearable sensing, sensorimotor interaction, and spatial interfaces.

With over six years of industry experience in software engineering and interaction design, I'm bringing together my background in web and mobile application development, real-time 3D development, and hardware prototyping to design and investigate new forms of XR interaction. My goal is to make immersive experiences more intuitive, natural, and accessible.
