Hi! I'm Sushant, an Engineer and aspiring XR Researcher exploring how we can move beyond screens and controllers to create more natural and intuitive ways of interacting with virtual worlds.

I hold an M.S. in Information Studies from UT Austin, where I developed a strong interest in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and immersive technologies. With over six years of experience in software engineering and UX design, I've built a strong foundation in developing complex interactive systems, rapid prototyping, and translating human-centered design concepts into functional applications.

As I prepare for Fall 2027 PhD applications, I'm pursuing independent research in next-generation XR interfaces, bringing together real-time 3D development, wearable sensing, haptics, and physical computing. I hope to bring this multidisciplinary experience to an XR research lab, contributing to the design and development of novel interaction techniques, building experimental hardware and software prototypes, and evaluating how people perceive and interact with immersive environments.
